🔊 Echo sonar

 
Use your phone like a bat. The speaker sends a short chirp, the microphone hears the echo, and the delay tells you the distance: distance = speed of sound × time ÷ 2. Hold the phone 15 cm to 2 m from a flat object like a wall or a book. Quiet room works best.

📷 Camera light sensor

 
Use your phone camera as a real light sensor. The brightness it sees controls the light sensor (LDR) in your circuit. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
